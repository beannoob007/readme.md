At a glance
Elo Catalyst does not collect, sell or share personal data. It has no internet access, so the games you load and the reports you create cannot leave your phone through the app.

No account
There is nothing to sign up for and no login.
No internet access
The app does not request the internet permission, so it cannot connect to any server.
No ads, analytics or tracking
No advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software is included.
Your games stay on your phone
Games are analysed on your device by the built-in chess engine.
1
Who this policy covers
This policy applies to the Android app Elo Catalyst (package com.navinkarki.elocatalyst), published on Google Play by Navin Karki, an independent developer. In this policy, “we” means the developer and “you” means the person using the app.

2
Information the app handles
The app works only with what you give it, and it keeps that information on your device:

Information	Where it stays	Shared with anyone?
Chess games you load (PGN files or pasted text)	In the app’s memory while it is open. Not stored, not uploaded.	No
Analysis results (accuracy, flagged moves, opening statistics)	In memory; cleared by “Start over” or when the app closes.	No
PDF reports you create	In the app’s private storage on your device.	Only if you tap Save or Share, and then only to the place or app you choose.
Settings (analysis depth, playback speed, the Stockfish version you chose)	On your device.	No
Feedback e-mail (optional)	A draft in your own e-mail app.	Only if you send it yourself (see section 5).
Player names inside a PGN file are part of the game record you chose to load. The app uses them only on your device, to let you pick a player and to label the analysis.

3
What the app does not do
It does not create accounts or ask for your name, e-mail address or phone number.
It does not connect to the internet, so it sends nothing to us or to anyone else.
It does not use the Android Advertising ID, and it contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software.
It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or other files.
It does not sell, rent or trade any information.
4
Permissions
Elo Catalyst asks for no permissions that you would see or approve. Files are opened through Android’s own file picker or the “Open with” and “Share” menus, which give the app access only to the file you select.

5
Feedback by e-mail
On the “About the developer” screen you can tap Send feedback. The app then opens a draft in your own e-mail app, addressed to the developer, with the app version, the Android version and the device model filled in to help understand a problem. You can edit or delete anything in the draft, and nothing is sent unless you send it yourself. If you do, we receive your message and your e-mail address, use them only to reply and to improve the app, and delete them when they are no longer needed.

6
Third parties
Chess engine. The analysis is done by Stockfish, open-source software that runs inside the app on your device. It has no network access.
Links. Links on the app’s licence screens (for example to source code on GitHub) open in your web browser. The website you visit and your browser are covered by their own privacy policies.
Google Play. When you download or update the app, Google handles that under the Google Privacy Policy. Google gives every developer anonymous, combined statistics such as install counts, ratings and crash rates. These statistics do not identify you.
7
Children
Elo Catalyst is a general chess-analysis tool and is not directed at children under 13. The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

8
How long information is kept, and how to delete it
Games and analysis results are kept in memory only and disappear when you choose “Start over” or close the app.
PDF reports and settings stay on your device until you clear the app’s storage (Android Settings → Apps → Elo Catalyst → Storage) or uninstall the app.
Reports you saved or shared elsewhere stay where you put them.
Feedback e-mails you send are deleted when they are no longer needed. You can ask us to delete yours at any time.
9
Security
Because the app keeps your information on your device, it is protected by Android’s app sandbox and your device’s own lock and encryption. The app turns off Android’s automatic cloud backup for its data, and the chess engine runs in an isolated component that cannot access the network or your files.

10
